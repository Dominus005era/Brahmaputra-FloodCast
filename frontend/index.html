System Methodology & Scientific Architecture

How Brahmaputra FloodCast Forecasts River Surges

The Brahmaputra River basin is one of the most hydrologically active river systems on Earth. FloodCast integrates hydrodynamic station measurements with predictive machine learning classifiers to bridge the critical gap between observation and emergency action.

The Five-Stage Early Warning Pipeline

STAGE 01 • SENSE

Continuous Sensor Telemetry

Hydrological gauge stations record instantaneous river stage (meters above mean sea level) at calibrated cross-sections. Telemetry is sampled continuously and bundled into hourly time series.

STAGE 02 • INGEST

Automated Ingestion & Feature Derivation

Raw sensor feeds pass through automated validation routines to filter gauge noise and transmission dropouts. Rolling features are engineered in real time: 3-hour stage differentials (\(\Delta H / \Delta t\)), 6-hour moving averages, and upstream catchment precipitation accumulations.

STAGE 03 • PREDICT

Machine Learning Classification (+6h Lead Time)

A trained Gradient Boosting / Random Forest ensemble evaluates current hydrological vectors to compute the probability of river stage exceeding the critical threshold (60.00m) within the next 6 hours. Model validation yields 94.2% test accuracy and balanced precision/recall metrics.

STAGE 04 • CALIBRATE

Threshold Calibration & Risk Stratification

Model probabilities are mapped to institutional risk categories: LOW (<40%), MODERATE (40–69%), HIGH (70–89%), and CRITICAL (≥90%). Risk levels are cross-referenced with local embankment geometries to determine overtopping hazard.

STAGE 05 • ALERT

Emergency Protocol & Multi-Tier Escalation

Escalation directives trigger automatically based on calculated severity: NONE (routine), LOCAL (Circle Officer & Panchayat alert), DISTRICT (District Disaster Management Authority DEOC mobilization), or STATE (Assam State Disaster Management Authority - ASDMA incident management).

Five-Stage Early Warning Architecture

Comprehensive technical breakdown of telemetry acquisition, feature engineering, and decision support

01 • IN SITU TELEMETRY

Ground Sensor & Satellite Stream Integration

Hydro-acoustic and pressure-transducer river sensors installed at NH15 Fakirpara record instantaneous gauge heights every 60 minutes. Sourced concurrently through national hydrometric databases (NWDP) and global catchment models (GLOFAS).

02 • FEATURE ENGINEERING & NORMALIZATION

Hydrological State Vector Generation

Raw sensor measurements are enriched with temporal derivative features: 3-hour delta, 6-hour rolling mean, rate of stage acceleration, and seasonal baseline variances. Features are standardized to prevent distribution drift during peak monsoon cycles.

03 • MACHINE LEARNING INFERENCE

6-Hour High-Water Probability Modeling

A supervised ensemble classifier evaluates the state vector to produce a calibrated likelihood of the river breaching the 60.00m danger threshold 6 hours into the future. Test verification demonstrates 94.2% accuracy on historic Assam flood seasons.

04 • MULTI-TIER ESCALATION MAPPING

Disaster Authority Routing

Inference outcomes directly dictate the operational posture: NORMAL (baseline observation), ADVISORY (Circle Officer inspection), WARNING (DDMA deployment), or CRITICAL (ASDMA state command center activation).

About Brahmaputra FloodCast

Brahmaputra FloodCast is a specialized hydrological forecasting platform developed to mitigate annual flood disruptions across Assam's Brahmaputra River system.

By fusing telemetry from the CWC / NWDP gauge stations with real-time river gradient modeling and ensemble machine learning classifiers, the platform gives disaster authorities an actionable 6-hour advance window before high-water stages breach danger levels.
