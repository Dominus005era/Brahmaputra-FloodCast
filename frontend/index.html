FloodSense AI Architecture & Methodology

Comprehensive overview of algorithms, data sources, and rating curves.

1. Dual-Stream Telemetry Buffer

Ground river sensors are monitored every minute. When government NWDP sensors publish new gauge levels, they are ingested directly. If sensor dropouts exceed 3 hours, FloodSense AI activates its Hydrology Bridge using ECMWF GloFAS discharge models and Open-Meteo precipitation feeds.

2. 19-Feature Temporal Lag Space

Rather than relying solely on the current height, the model analyzes historical momentum: 1h, 3h, 6h, 12h, and 24h water lags; 6h, 12h, and 24h moving averages; 24h peaks and troughs; and rate-of-rise deltas.

3. 200-Tree Random Forest Model

Trained on thousands of chronological hourly observations from Fakirpara Tangni station. Verified with 98.56% test accuracy and 99.1% F1-score on strict holdout validation sets with zero lookahead bias.

4. Automated Disaster Escalation

Model predictions are mapped to operational response levels: LOW (Routine), MODERATE (Local Circle Officer), HIGH (District DDMA), and CRITICAL (State ASDMA emergency response).
