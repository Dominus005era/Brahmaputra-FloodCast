  • No public notifications or administrative mobilization required.

  • Advise riverside livestock owners to avoid low char grazing.

  • Activate Darrang District Disaster Management Authority (DDMA).
  • Pre-position SDRF rescue boats and food relief packages.
  • Continuous 24-hour dyke surveillance for seepage or erosion along NH-15.

State Disaster Authority (ASDMA) Emergency Directive

  • Immediate evacuation of endangered villages into pre-designated relief shelters.
  • Direct deployment of National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) battalions.
  • Traffic regulation on NH-15 bridge crossing to prevent structural overloading.

System Specifications & ML Engineering Architecture

Technical foundation of the 6-hour advance hydrological forecasting model.

19 Feature Engineering Pipeline

  • Current Stage: Observed water level ($h_t$).
  • Temporal: Month, Hour of Day, Day of Year.
  • Lag Observations: $h_{t-1h}, h_{t-3h}, h_{t-6h}, h_{t-12h}, h_{t-24h}$.
  • Rolling Moving Averages: 6h, 12h, 24h rolling means.
  • Extreme Envelopes: 24h rolling max and min values.
  • Rate-of-Rise (Δh): 1h, 3h, 6h, 12h, 24h level changes.
